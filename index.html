How to Play

1
Stand where the camera sees you

Head and shoulders is enough. Step back if you can — then your legs count too.

2
Copy the figure

Follow along at your own pace. You do not need to match it exactly.

3
Read the words above the guide

The big words say what the movement is. The character on the left shows you how.

4
Rest whenever you like

Sitting down works too. A helper can start it for you at any time.
